## Copy block (paste into the Earn form)

**Name:** Pacto

**Tagline:** Group buys, without the trust fall.

**Short description (under 300 characters):** Pacto is escrow for group orders. Fans and friends pool USDC into an on-chain vault, and the organizer is paid only as goods arrive. If the order never ships, every buyer reclaims their share automatically. Every order is also a Blink, so "join" works inside X and Discord.

**Long description:** Group orders are how fandom communities in Latin America buy merch, sneakers and hauls together. Today they run on Pix screenshots and the organizer's reputation, and when someone disappears with the money, nobody can get it back. Pacto replaces that trust with a Solana program. Buyers join with USDC and can leave for a full refund while the order is open. When the minimum is met, the organizer locks the order and receives an advance to place the bulk order. Each buyer confirms receipt to release their remaining share, unconfirmed shares auto-release after the delivery window, disputes go to an arbiter, and if the organizer never ships, buyers reclaim the remainder. Completed orders become a permanent, portable organizer track record.

**Chains and tools:** Solana (devnet), Anchor, USDC (SPL), Solana Actions and Blinks, Next.js 16, Tailwind, Solana Wallet Adapter, LiteSVM tests.
