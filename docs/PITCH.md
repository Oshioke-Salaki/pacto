# Pitch and demo scripts

## Pitch video (2 to 3 minutes)

**0:00 Cold open (15s).** "In September, about a thousand BTS fans in Bahia sent R$ 460,000 over Pix to one organizer for light sticks. The light sticks never came, and neither did the money. This month, 800 fans in Lima lost the same way. This happens every tour, in every country in Latin America."

**0:15 The mechanic (30s).** Group orders: one organizer, many small Pix payments, a bulk order abroad, re-shipping weeks later. It is how fandom buys. It runs on screenshots of receipts and trust. Pix is instant and irreversible, and buyer protection does not cover a transfer to a person.

**0:45 Pacto (45s).** Pacto is escrow shaped like a group order. Buyers pay into a Solana vault, not a wallet. When the minimum is met the organizer gets a capped advance to place the order. The rest releases per buyer as items arrive. Never shipped? Buyers reclaim. Item missing? A neutral arbiter decides. Every finished order becomes the organizer's permanent track record. Show the order page: terms visible before paying, the timeline, the organizer card.

**1:30 Why Solana (20s).** Sub-cent fees make per-buyer settlement on R$ 40 items possible. Finality in seconds matches Pix. Every order is a Blink, so the join button lives inside the X thread where the order was announced.

**1:50 Business (25s).** 1.5 to 2.5 percent on organizer payouts, enforced on-chain. Escrow norms are 1 to 3 percent; marketplaces take 10 to 20. On-ramp spread and an organizer Pro tier follow. The same contract covers sneaker drops, Shein hauls, class trips and any peer-to-peer deal.

**2:15 Ask and next (20s).** Mainnet with a multisig admin, Pix on-ramp partner, Portuguese and Spanish, embedded wallets. We start with the most organized, most online, most scammed buyers in the region, and they already recruit each other.

## Demo video (up to 3 minutes)

Two browser profiles or two wallets: Organizer (A) and Buyer (B). Devnet.

1. (0:00) Landing page scroll: headline, the four steps, the stats with sources.
2. (0:20) A: Start a group order. Fill title, price 40 USDC, min 2, max 10, advance 30 percent, 30 day ship window, 14 day delivery window. Create. Wallet signs. Lands on the order page.
3. (0:50) Copy the Blink. Open dial.to preview with B connected. Join 1 unit from the Blink. Back on the order page: progress moved, B appears in buyers.
4. (1:15) B on the web app: join 1 more unit. Show "Your position" with paid and held amounts. Show the buyer protection terms expanded.
5. (1:35) A: minimum reached. Lock and receive advance. Show the organizer USDC balance change and the explorer link. Status: Ordering.
6. (1:55) A: Mark as shipped with a tracking code. Status: Shipped.
7. (2:10) B: Confirm I received it. Toast with tx link. Status: Completed. Organizer page shows 1 completed, 0 disputes lost.
8. (2:30) Refund path on a second order: A cancels while open, B claims a full refund. Close on the dashboard with next actions surfaced.

Keep every transaction toast on screen long enough to read. Show the explorer once.
