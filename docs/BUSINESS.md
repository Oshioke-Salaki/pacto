Structural reasons it keeps happening:
- Pix is instant and irreversible. Only about 8 percent of the money contested as Pix fraud is returned to victims through the official dispute mechanism (MED): R$ 1.4 billion recovered out of R$ 17.6 billion requested between November 2021 and June 2025 (8.2 percent, Banco Central data via O Globo), and 8.2 percent of requested amounts in 2024 ([TecMundo](https://www.tecmundo.com.br/seguranca/289538-ferramenta-banco-central-devolveu-r-1-1-bi-perdidos-pix.htm)). A Finsiders analysis of BC data reached the same 8 percent ([Finsiders](https://finsidersbrasil.com.br/noticias-sobre-fintechs/fraudes/mesmo-com-med-so-8-dos-valores-roubados-via-pix-sao-devolvidos/)). Pix fraud losses reported to the BC reached R$ 4.94 billion in 2024, up 70 percent on 2023 (BC data via Broadcast), and MED refund requests nearly doubled to R$ 10.2 billion ([Canaltech](https://canaltech.com.br/podcast/podcast-canaltech/med-do-pix-da-pra-recuperar-o-dinheiro-de-um-golpe-5891/)). Febraban's narrower bank-sector figure is R$ 2.7 billion.
- Group orders run on trust by design. Fanlore documents that GOMs scam or ghost buyers with little recourse because payments go through friends-and-family rails. [Fanlore](https://fanlore.org/wiki/Group_order)
- Mercado Pago's buyer protection does not cover Pix sent directly to a seller, which is how GOs are paid. [Mercado Pago](https://www.mercadopago.com.br/blog/o-que-e-compra-garantida-mercado-pago)

The same mechanics (one organizer, many small payments, delivery weeks later) cover sneaker drops, Shein and AliExpress hauls, fan-made merch, class trips, and festival camping groups. Fandom is the wedge, not the ceiling.

## 4. Competition

No escrow product built for group orders exists anywhere we could find. Adjacent players:

| Category | Players | What they do | What they miss |
|---|---|---|---|
| GO logistics | Kfriday, Delivered Korea, Paysable | Consolidation and shipping for organizers | Never hold buyer funds; trust problem untouched |
| Payment intermediaries (BR) | Mercado Pago, PagSeguro | Buyer protection for card and in-platform payments, 4 to 5 percent fees | No coverage for Pix-to-person, no group logic, no organizer reputation |
| Western escrow | Escrow.com (2.6 percent under USD 5k, USD 50 minimum), Trustap (3 to 4.4 percent plus fixed) | One buyer, one seller | Not built for 60 buyers and one organizer; minimums kill R$ 80 tickets |
| Group buying | Pinduoduo, Temu team purchase | Discount for recruiting friends | Platform is the seller; not applicable to community-run orders |

Sources: [Escrow.com fees](https://www.escrow.com/fee-calculator), [Trustap fees](https://trustap.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/41229698897169-Pricing-and-fees-how-do-Trustap-fees-work), [Kfriday](https://kfriday.net/post/how-to-become-a-gom-and-how-kfriday-can-help).

## 5. How Pacto makes money

| Stream | Mechanism | Benchmark |
|---|---|---|
| Protocol fee | 1.5 to 2.5 percent of each organizer payout, enforced on-chain (`Config.fee_bps`). Buyers pay nothing extra. | Escrow norms are 1 to 3 percent; marketplaces take 10 to 20 percent (Shopee BR 14 to 20 percent, Mercado Livre 10 to 19 percent). |
| On-ramp spread | Pix to USDC conversion through a licensed partner with a 0.5 to 1 percent spread shared with Pacto. | Card acquiring in Brazil averages 2.15 percent MDR. |
| Organizer Pro | Monthly subscription for verified badge, custom GO pages, bulk shipping tools, analytics, priority arbitration. | SaaS for sellers (Bling, Tiny) charge R$ 50 to 300 per month. |
| Float yield (later) | Opt-in yield on idle vault balances during the 2 to 8 week GO window, conservative venues only, shared with organizers. | Fintech float economics; requires risk disclosure. |
| Escrow API (later) | Third-party marketplaces, Discord bots and creator tools call Pacto for programmable escrow. | Stripe-style usage pricing. |

Unit economics at launch: a typical GO of 60 buyers at R$ 150 each is R$ 9,000 of volume. At 2 percent, Pacto earns R$ 180 per GO with near-zero marginal cost (Solana fees under USD 0.01 per settlement). Costs scale with arbitration labor, which the reputation system is designed to minimize.

## 6. Why now

- Pix made instant payment universal and made instant fraud universal with it.
- USDC on Solana has Pix on-ramps live (Circle enabled BRL to USDC via Pix in 2024; Sphere, Transfero and others operate ramps).
- Blinks make "join this GO" a link that renders as a pay button inside X, which is where GOs are already recruited.
- Brazil's VASP regime took effect in February 2026, so a compliant path for stablecoin payments exists.

## 7. Regulatory posture (Brazil)

Pacto's program is non-custodial: funds sit in on-chain vaults controlled by code, not by Pacto. Pix collection and BRL custody will run through an authorized payment institution partner rather than a Pacto license, because the BCB raised minimum capital for payment institutions to R$ 9.2 to 32.8 million in late 2025 and moved authorization deadlines forward. Sources: [Lets Money](https://www.letsmoney.com.br/regulacao/capital-minimo-fintechs-banco-central-novas-regras/), [Levy Salomao](https://www.levysalomao.com.br/publicacoes/artigo/instituicoes-de-pagamento-quando-pedir-autorizacao). Legal review is required before mainnet.

## 8. The investor pitch in five lines

1. Fans in Latin America send hundreds of millions of reais a year to strangers to buy merch together, and when it goes wrong, nothing comes back.
2. Pacto puts that money in a Solana vault that only pays the organizer as items arrive, and turns every completed order into portable reputation.
3. No one has built escrow shaped like a group order. The incumbents protect card payments on their own marketplaces and ignore the Pix-to-person economy.
4. We earn 2 percent on volume with near-zero marginal cost, plus on-ramp and SaaS revenue, and the same contract extends to any group purchase and any peer-to-peer deal.
5. We start with the most organized, most online, most scammed buyers in the region, and they already recruit each other.
